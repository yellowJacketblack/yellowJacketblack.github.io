How the Exchange Works

  • The Prototype: You share your current project challenge with me. I will build a working prototype of your data pipeline or dashboard for your reference, so you can experience exactly what it is like to work with me.
  • The Portfolio: In return, I will display your project request on this page as a demonstration of my experience.
  • The Reference: You provide a reference detailing your experience as the client during this process.
